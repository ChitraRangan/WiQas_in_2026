WiQAS focus

Where quantum research meets application.

The symposium creates an interdisciplinary setting for conversations spanning quantum computing, sensing, cryptography, hardware, software, education, and data-intensive scientific applications.

  • ✓Academic and industry perspectives
  • ✓Invited talks and application-driven discussions
